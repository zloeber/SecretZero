$ secretzero providers
Usage: secretzero providers [OPTIONS] COMMAND [ARGS]...

  Manage and introspect providers.

  Providers are external systems (Vault, AWS, Azure, etc.) that store or help
  manage secrets. These commands let you discover available providers and
  their capabilities.

Options:
  --help  Show this message and exit.

Commands:
  capabilities  Show capabilities of a specific provider.
  list          List all registered providers.
  methods       List methods for a provider, optionally filtered by type.
  schema        Show schema/signature for a specific provider method.
  token-info    Show authentication token permissions and scopes.
